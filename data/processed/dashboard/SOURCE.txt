Dashboard data: derived from the Retailrocket recommender system dataset
Provider: Retailrocket / Roman Zykov
Source: https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/retailrocket/ecommerce-dataset

Changes made: the parquet files in this folder were derived from events.csv (original event log) and the category
history extracted from the dataset's item_properties files. Exact duplicate rows were removed, events at or after
2015-09-15 21:00 UTC were dropped, product categories were joined as of each event's time, and the events were
restructured into visitor-level tables (first view, 24-hour funnel, daily activity). See analysis/19_export_dashboard_data.py
and the project's preprocessing notes for the exact steps. No original file is redistributed.
